About the Doctor

Dr. Gogineni Ratnakara Rao is a distinguished pulmonologist and interventional respiratory specialist with advanced training from India's premier medical institutions.

He completed his MD in General Medicine from AIIMS, New Delhi, securing AIR 11, and subsequently earned his DM in Pulmonary & Critical Care Medicine from PGIMER, Chandigarh, securing AIR 1. These achievements reflect a strong academic foundation and a commitment to excellence in respiratory medicine.

With expertise in asthma, COPD, interstitial lung disease (ILD), tuberculosis, sleep medicine, bronchoscopy, EBUS, pleural diseases, critical care and advanced interventional pulmonology, he provides comprehensive, evidence-based care for patients with simple to complex respiratory disorders.

He currently serves as Senior Consultant, Clinical & Interventional Pulmonary Medicine at Apollo Hospitals, Financial District, Hyderabad.
